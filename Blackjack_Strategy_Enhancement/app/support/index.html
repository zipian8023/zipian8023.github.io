开始一场模拟

“离线孪生”支持一至六门。设置会话标签与模拟初始资金后创建会话；所有注额均为没有现金价值的模拟筹码。可手动行动、单步或自动运行，暂停后点击手牌、牌靴、牌盒、CSM 查看实际状态。关闭详情不会发牌。正常界面隐藏未揭露牌；“调试·研究透视”只检查模拟器自产牌，关闭详情即退出。

设置与本地策略

设置保留“基本参数／最佳策略／边赛”三个页签。修改需保存才生效，取消或放弃修改恢复已保存状态。默认策略只读，可使用本地可编辑副本。已保存策略用于孪生模拟；规则不匹配时，内置快照查询会说明原因。

已开放与尚未开放的玩法

已实现 ±13 与对子。幸运七、保险、规划中的本地训练尚未开放；幸运七预留设置不产生游戏或结算。已有的单手练习仍可使用。

历史记录与结果

已结算牌局、标签与回放记录保存在本机。分析使用真实保存的模拟结算；净收益与可用余额不同，活动注额在结算前占用资金。没有参加对应边注时，边注筛选可能没有样本。

Apple Watch 课程

Watch 的 Quick Strategy 使用离线目录；最近课程与练习摘要只显示在 iPhone 练习页检查并明确保存的课程，交付取决于配对与 WatchConnectivity 状态，不同步当前孪生桌面。课程“最近／过期”仅描述保存时间。

Read in English

Start a simulation

Open 离线孪生 (Offline Twin), choose one to six positions, enter a session label and virtual initial funds, then create a session. The app uses saved local rules. Wagers and balances are virtual chips with no cash value.

Use a legal action, advance one step, or start automatic play. Pause before inspecting a particular moment. Tap the dealer, a playing position, shoe, discard tray or CSM to see its current state; closing the detail keeps the same table. Hidden cards are concealed normally. The clearly labeled debug inspection switch reveals the simulator's own generated cards and internal order and resets when the detail closes.

Settings and strategy

The three configuration pages cover basic parameters, strategy and side bets. Changes are drafts until saved. Cancel/discard restores the saved state. The default strategy is read-only; create or choose a local copy to edit it. The saved local strategy drives the simulator. Unsupported or unmatched rule combinations can disable bundled lookup; restore a matching ruleset if the lookup says its snapshot does not apply.

±13 and pairs are implemented. Lucky Seven, insurance and the planned local training feature are unavailable. Reserved Lucky Seven controls do not enable gameplay or payouts. The separate existing single-hand Practice exercise is available.

History and results

Settled rounds, session tags and replay frames are stored locally. Analysis filters use actual saved settlements. Net profit is not the same as available balance: active wagers reserve funds until settlement. Side-bet charts can be empty when no matching side bet was played. Simulation results do not promise future results.

Apple Watch

Quick Strategy uses the bundled offline catalog. Last Lesson and Practice display the lesson explicitly saved after checking an answer in iPhone Practice. Open the companion on your paired Watch and save a checked lesson from iPhone. Delivery follows Apple's WatchConnectivity availability. Recent/Expired describes the lesson's age, not live-table freshness. The Watch does not show the current six-position simulation.
