数据收集

开发者不会通过本应用收集个人数据。当前版本没有广告或分析 SDK、跟踪、开发者账户或应用内购买。

设备上的记录

为继续模拟并进行复盘，应用会在设备上保存设置、本地策略编辑、投注预设、会话标签、模拟牌面与牌靴状态、动作历史、注额、结算、模拟余额和回放记录。查询与分析均在本机执行。请勿在会话标签中填写敏感个人信息。

配对 Apple Watch

只有在你明确保存已检查的练习课程时，应用才会通过 Apple WatchConnectivity 向配对 Apple Watch 发送规则集、模拟手牌、建议动作、策略快照标识、保存时间与可选练习得分。这些信息不会发送到开发者服务器，Watch 也不会收到当前孪生桌面或隐藏牌靴顺序。

保留与删除

本地记录会保留在你的设备中。删除应用会移除该设备上的应用数据；仅卸载以释放空间可能保留数据。Watch 可能保留独立的已保存课程，需要单独移除其应用数据。备份由操作系统及你的备份设置控制。开发者没有可删除或恢复的服务器副本。

平台与第三方服务

Apple 可根据其政策和你的设置处理商店分发、诊断和系统服务。打开支持或隐私网页链接后，适用对应网站的政策。开发者不会出售应用数据，也不会向广告方共享模拟记录。

自愿支持请求

支持渠道使用 Formspree：你主动提交的问题类型、消息、可选回复邮箱、应用版本和设备说明，以及用于查找该请求的随机编号，会由 Formspree 接收、存储并转发到开发者使用 Google 邮件服务的私有收件邮箱，用于处理请求。没有附件上传，也不自动提取应用记录。

Formspree 可能处理 IP 地址、浏览器类型、来源域名和访问时间等技术数据；验证可能使用 Google reCAPTCHA。有关信息可能在美国及服务商运营的其他地区处理，详见 Formspree 隐私政策及 Google 隐私政策。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，访问日志依 GitHub 隐私声明处理。此网站没有开发者添加的广告或访问分析代码。

开发者仅将支持通信用于处理你的请求。Formspree 免费方案提供 30 天后台归档窗口；这不是邮件或服务日志的自动删除期限。转发邮件保留在开发者收件邮箱中，直至开发者删除；服务商可能按各自政策保留运行、安全或法定义务所需记录。你可通过支持表单申请查询、更正或删除开发者持有的支持通信；请提供可识别该请求的信息，不要再次发送密码、私钥或私人数据库。

Data stays on your devices

The developer does not collect personal data through this app. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs, tracking, developer accounts or in-app purchases in the current version.

The app stores information locally so you can continue a simulation and review it later. This can include settings, edited strategy tables, betting presets, session labels, simulated cards and shoe state, action history, wagers, results, virtual balances and saved replay records. Hand lookups and analysis run on the device. Do not put sensitive personal information into session labels.

Apple Watch

When you explicitly save a checked practice lesson, the app can send it to your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. The lesson contains its ruleset, simulated hand, suggested action, strategy snapshot identity, save time and optional practice score. It is not sent to a developer server. The Watch does not receive the simulator's current table or hidden shoe order.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your devices until removed. Deleting the app removes that device's app data; offloading the app may retain data. A paired Watch may retain its own saved lesson until its app data is removed. Any device backups are controlled by your operating-system and backup settings. The developer has no server-side copy to delete or restore.

Third-party services

Apple may process App Store distribution, diagnostics and platform services according to its own policies and your settings. Opening a support or privacy web link leaves the local app workflow and is subject to the website's policies. The developer does not sell app data or share simulation records with advertisers.

Optional support requests

The support channel uses Formspree to receive, store and forward the topic, message, optional reply email, app version, device details and a random request reference you submit to the developer’s private inbox, hosted by Google, to handle your request. It accepts no attachments and does not extract app records.

Formspree may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, referring domain and access time. Verification may use Google reCAPTCHA. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where the providers operate. See the Formspree Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. GitHub Pages hosts this website and handles access logs under its privacy statement. The developer has added no advertising or visitor analytics scripts.

The developer uses support correspondence only to handle your request. Formspree’s free plan provides a 30-day dashboard archive window, which is not an automatic deletion deadline for email or service logs. Forwarded messages remain in the developer’s inbox until deleted by the developer. Providers may retain records needed for operations, security or legal duties under their policies. Use the support form to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence held by the developer, with enough information to identify the request. Do not include credentials, private keys or private databases.
